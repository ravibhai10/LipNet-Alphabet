# Fresh-participant external acceptance protocol

No new consenting participants are available in this mission. This document defines a procedure; it reports no new recognition results. Software fixtures, synthetic mouth crops and warm-up tensors are never participant evidence.

1. Obtain institutional approval where required, documented source permission and dataset license terms before recruitment or reuse. Record the permission reference, permitted purposes, retention, withdrawal contact and reviewer identity. Public availability does not establish redistribution or biometric-training permission. Do not copy protected datasets into Docker images or publication packages.
2. Recruit genuinely new participants. Exclude exposed historical test speakers and resolve aliases through an independent reviewer. Assign stable participant IDs separate from authentication tokens. Do not upload identity documents into the recording store. Record only the minimum permission metadata needed.
3. Freeze supported vocabulary, model/checkpoint hashes, preprocessing, rejection policy and evaluation plan before final test access. Preserve the eight-word reference. Keep alphabet, LRW500, twenty-word and sentence claims separate. Full A–Z validation requires authentic W evidence; GRID's missing W cannot be replaced by fixtures.
4. For population generalization, hold out participants completely. For per-user adaptation, use that user's distinct train, validation and final-test sessions; label the claim personal cross-session, never speaker-independent. Collect different lighting, session dates, camera hardware and capture provenance without silently merging strata.
5. Collect genuine physical-webcam clips and permissioned prerecorded replay as separate strata. Store original video plus timed source samples, independently measured quality, blur/corruption decisions, usable-frame ratio and correspondence results. Quarantine borderline clips for documented review. Delete unusable media and retain only deletion receipts. Record exclusions and denominator changes.
6. Screen exact and near duplicates across the complete collection and its splits. Do not count repeated encodings as independent utterances. Independently review annotation correctness, identity and session assignments. Freeze immutable source/ROI hashes, labels, negative records and manifests before fitting.
7. Fit only training data. Choose architecture, policy and thresholds only on validation. Include authentic isolated out-of-vocabulary words, silence/no-speech, bad framing and phrase negatives separately. Phrase negatives alone do not validate unseen isolated words. Establish sample sizes and uncertainty intervals before data collection; the software minimum of two clips per class/session and 20 negatives is not a statistical guarantee.
8. Final test access is one immutable attempt per candidate. Resume only with the same checkpoint, manifest, preprocessing and policy after a crash; retain access receipts and lock ownership. No retuning from final-test results. Use fresh final data for a changed candidate.
9. Report raw top-1, accepted-subset accuracy, coverage, rejection rate, false acceptance with negative denominators, per-class recall, confusion matrix and uncertainty intervals together. Include missing/failed recordings and abstentions. Never substitute top-5 for top-3. Mark confidence as uncalibrated unless calibration is independently measured.
10. Measure end-to-end request latency, encoder time, startup/load time, peak memory, CPU threads, queued/busy counts, camera configuration and host specifications on each actual demonstration machine. State warm/cold conditions and sample counts. Do not treat this laptop or one smoke latency as a final-hardware compatibility promise.
11. Test ordinary reference and explicitly selected per-user candidates through the actual HTTP and browser paths. Verify owner isolation, consent renewal, expiry/restart/retry, withdrawal invalidation/fallback, deleted derivatives and rollback refusal. Complete multi-tab/browser-cache acceptance before claiming frontend integration.
12. Obtain independent sign-off with exact artifact hashes, commands, environment lock, results, exclusions, license/permission references and remaining limitations. Keep private participant media out of public reports.

Acceptance remains pending for independent physical-webcam recognition, complete A–Z, genuine licensed LRW500, isolated OOV rejection and population/speaker-independent generalization. No completion or accuracy claim may be inferred from model loadability, source-code coverage, a passing test suite or this protocol.
