E. AI/ML Problems
Alphabet model
Verified architecture:
[B,T,1,140,46] → three framewise Conv2D stages → one bidirectional LSTM, hidden size 96 → Linear 192→128→26
This is isolated classification, not CTC spelling or sentence decoding.
Important weaknesses:
models/training_summary.json records 12% test accuracy, but the protected checkpoint does not contain enough training/speaker provenance to reconstruct that experiment independently.
GRID supplies 25 letters, excluding W. A 26-output head does not prove 26 learned classes.
Current browser crops are square aligned mouth ROIs resized to a tall 140×46 tensor. Shape compatibility does not establish compatibility with the checkpoint’s original training distribution.
Runtime .5 confidence and .05 margin thresholds are heuristic.
Browser stabilization does not establish accuracy or calibration.
models/alphabet_model.py:73 uses the last BiLSTM output. The reverse component at that position does not summarize the whole sequence as the final reverse hidden state would.
Spatial pooling reduces features to 1×1 before expanding them back to 4×4. This repeats spatial values and warrants an ablation in a future retrained model.
Live cross-speaker, lighting, head-pose, occlusion, and camera generalization remain unmeasured.
Verdict: a loadable experimental alphabet classifier, not a verified dependable A–Z webcam recognizer.

Personal adaptation: what is actually implemented
There are two substantive word workflows:
pipeline/word_recognition/personalized.py:
Fits a new logistic head using personal recordings plus original MIRACL training features.
Selects on personal validation sessions.
Performs a separate paired test.
Is personal cross-session evaluation, not speaker-independent evaluation.
pipeline/adaptive/training.py:
Fits a new logistic head on reviewed, frozen feedback training records.
Compares with the champion on validation.
Uses separate one-time final evaluation.
Controls promotion through reviewer approval and saved gates.
Alphabet adaptation fine-tunes the CNN-BiLSTM in alphabet_training.py.
Therefore:
Prototype/centroid adaptation: not the active method in these inspected workflows.
Classifier refitting: implemented for words.
Encoder fine-tuning: not performed by the word adaptation.
Alphabet fine-tuning: implemented.
Vocabulary expansion: not supplied by the fixed eight-word adaptive trainer.
Full foundation-model retraining: not implemented here.
A single recording cannot immediately replace the private champion through the normal workflow. Dataset freezing requires at least two approved examples per class in each partition; promotion also requires negative evaluation and other gates.
However:
The adaptive champion is global per task, not per user.
Collectors’ recordings can contribute to a pooled reviewer-created dataset.
New word fitting does not automatically retain original MIRACL training examples.
Catastrophic regression on older populations remains possible despite paired new-validation checks.

F. Hidden Loopholes
A nonexistent evaluation can appear successful.
Unknown evaluation identifiers return benchmark constants.
Historical evidence can masquerade as current validation.
Dataset validation does not even require the files it claims to verify.
Ordinary checkpoint status can claim loaded models without loading them.
main.py:675, main.py:700, and the return at :709 contain misleading flags.
Per-user media ownership does not imply per-user learning.
Adaptive champions are task-global.
A reviewer identity check is procedural.
Speaker IDs are self-declared. Renaming a participant can bypass speaker-disjoint assumptions.
Exact duplicate detection is not near-duplicate detection.
A small pixel change or altered capture can evade full-sequence hashes.
Native video and PNG samples are not proven to show the same utterance.
Both are checked and hashed, but correspondence is not established.
Audio absence is declared rather than independently verified.
The stored metadata correctly admits this limitation.
“Integration passed” is weaker than its name.
training.py:301 sets it from a nonempty, finite evaluation result. That is not a deployed challenger browser/API test.
Successful regression can coexist with a failed scientific protocol.

Private SQLite reads acquire write-oriented transactions.

S. Final Engineering Verdict
Current maturity: an extensive research prototype with one reproducible limited-vocabulary result, substantial testing infrastructure, and useful controlled-feedback scaffolding. It also contains incompatible generations of implementation and misleading evidence presentation.
Demo readiness: conditional. A supervised eight-word research demonstration is plausible. Dependable alphabet recognition, arbitrary speech recognition and demonstrated personalization are not established.
Research credibility: strongest where the project preserves source IDs, feature hashes, classifier parameters and per-clip predictions. Weakest where constants or legacy diagnostics are presented as verified results.
Biggest weakness: the gap between measured offline classification and the capabilities implied by parts of the application.
Highest-value next improvement: one truthful, frozen, end-to-end physical-webcam evaluation—not another model or dashboard.
Lead-engineer recommendation: narrow the supported demonstration to the preserved eight-word model plus transparent feedback collection. Correct the evidence and security defects before adding capability.
